02 • UNDERSTAND (Minto Pyramid + Story)
Minto Main Answer:

Building isliye safe khadi rehti hai kyunki uska RCC skeleton saare wajan ko ek fixed Load Path se zameen mein bhejta hai: Slab ➔ Beam ➔ Column ➔ Foundation ➔ Soil.

Tray Analogy: Jaise aap haath mein bhari tray pakadte ho—Tray ki plate Slab hai, aapki baazu Beam hai, aapki spine aur pair Column hain, aur aapke joote Foundation hain jo aapko mitti mein dhasne nahi dete!

03 • LEARN (MECE Loads & Indian Codes)
1. Dead Load (IS 875 Part 1)

Building ka permanent wajan: Concrete slabs, beams, walls, aur terrace par rakha Chiller.

2. Live Load (IS 875 Part 2)

Movable wajan: Meridian ke 1,000 log, desks, chairs, aur parking ki cars (Live Load).

3. Lateral Load (IS 875 Pt 3 & IS 1893)

Sideways dhakka: Jaipur ki aandhi (Wind) aur Earthquake (Seismic Zone II), jise Shear Wall rokti hai.

04 • APPLY (D2O Consultancy Connection)
🌱 LEED LCA & Embodied Carbon

Building ke total Embodied Carbon ka 65%+ hissa Concrete aur Steel mein hota hai. D2O consultant BOQ lekar Fly Ash / GGBS mix suggest karta hai.

⚠️ Beam Depth vs. HVAC Duct Clash (5 Whys)

Agar Beam 650mm deep hai, toh AC duct nikalne ki jagah nahi bachegi! Isliye Clear Height aur BIM clash check GFC stage se pehle zaroori hai.
