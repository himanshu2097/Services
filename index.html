02 • UNDERSTAND (Minto Pyramid + Story Analogy)
Minto Main Answer First:

Meridian ki G+8 building isliye safe khadi rehti hai kyunki uska RCC skeleton har floor ke wajan ko ek fixed chain (Load Path) se zameen mein bhejta hai: Slab → Beam → Column → Foundation → Soil.

Hinglish Tray Analogy: Socho aapne haath mein ek bhari tray pakdi hai. Tray ki plate aapki Slab hai. Aapke haath aur baazu jo us tray ko neeche se sahara de rahe hain woh Beam hain. Aapki reedh ki haddi (spine) aur pair jo poora wajan seedha neeche le jaate hain woh Column hain. Aur aapke chaurhe joote jo aapko mitti mein dhasne se bachate hain woh Foundation hain!

03 • LEARN (MECE Technical Breakdown & IS Codes)

MECE Breakdown of Building Loads (IS 875 & IS 1893)

1. Dead Load (IS 875 Part 1)

Building ka apna permanent wajan: Concrete slabs, beams, brick walls, floor tiles, aur छत par rakha heavy Chiller.

2. Live Load (IS 875 Part 2)

Movable wajan: Meridian ke 1,000 employees, unke desks, chairs, aur basement mein khadi gaadiyan (Live Load).

3. Lateral Load (IS 875 Pt 3 / IS 1893)

Side se lagne wala dhakka: Jaipur ki tez aandhi (Wind) aur Earthquake (Seismic Zone II), jise Shear Wall rokti hai.

04 • APPLY (D2O Consultancy & Real Workflow)

Why a D2O Green Building Consultant Must Check Structure

1. LEED LCA & Embodied Carbon

Building ke total Embodied Carbon ka 65%+ hissa Concrete aur Steel mein hota hai. D2O consultant BOQ check karke Cement mein Fly Ash / GGBS mix suggest karta hai for LEED MR credits.

2. Beam Depth vs. HVAC Duct Clash (5 Whys)

Agar floor height 4.0m hai aur Beam 650mm deep hai, toh AC duct nikalne ki jagah nahi bachegi! Site par beam core-cutting se bachne ke liye design stage par Clear Height coordination zaroori hai.
